# Privacy Policy – Raspored časova

_Effective 27 October 2026_

Published at https://mcbane23.github.io/privacy/raspored-casova/ (source: the `mcbane23/privacy` repository).
When this policy changes, update both copies.

Raspored časova ("the app") is developed by Bane (GitHub: mcbane23). This policy explains what the app does with
your data.

## Data the app stores

Everything you enter (children's names, school, timetable, subjects, bell times, calendar events, extracurricular
activities with their times, places and notes, alarm and reminder settings) is
stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. The app has no account, no server and no analytics.

## Data the app collects or shares

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data, and it contains no ads or
third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not request internet access.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** – used only to show the "classes are over", morning, "pack the bag" and activity reminders you turn on in
  the app.
- **Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)** – used only so those reminders arrive at the exact minute.
- **Run at startup** – used only to set the reminders again after the phone restarts or the time changes.
- **Camera** – used only while you scan a timetable QR code shared from another phone. Camera images are not
  saved or sent anywhere.

## Sharing a timetable

When you choose "Prikaži QR kod", the app shows the child's name, school, timetable, bell times, shift settings,
calendar events, subject list and extracurricular activities as a QR code on your screen. Nothing leaves the phone unless someone scans that
code; sharing happens only between the two phones.

## Backups

If Android's own device backup is enabled on your phone, the system may include the app's data in your personal
backup (e.g. your Google account backup). This is handled by Android, not by the app.

## Deleting your data

You can delete a child or any entry inside the app. To delete all data, uninstall the app or clear its storage in
Android settings.

## Children

The app is made for primary-school children (about 6 to 15 years old) and their parents, and children may use it on
their own. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children: there are no accounts, no ads, no
analytics and no internet access, and nothing a child enters ever leaves the device unless a parent or the child
shows a QR code that another phone scans. The app follows Google Play's Families policy.

# Politika privatnosti – Raspored časova (srpski)

Sve što uneseš u aplikaciju (imena dece, škola, raspored, predmeti, zvona, događaji, vanškolske aktivnosti, alarm i
podsetnici) čuva se **samo na
tvom telefonu**. Aplikacija nema nalog, server ni analitiku, ne šalje i ne deli nikakve podatke i ne koristi
internet. Dozvola za obaveštenja služi samo za podsetnike o kraju nastave, jutarnji podsetnik, pakovanje torbe i aktivnosti, a dozvola za pokretanje pri uključivanju
telefona samo da se podsetnici vrate posle restarta; dozvola za alarme i podsetnike služi da stignu u minut. Kamera se koristi samo dok skeniraš QR kod rasporeda sa
drugog telefona; slike se ne čuvaju niti šalju. Podaci se brišu deinstalacijom aplikacije ili brisanjem
njene memorije u podešavanjima telefona. Aplikacija je namenjena deci osnovnoškolskog uzrasta i njihovim roditeljima;
ni od koga, pa ni od dece, ne prikuplja lične podatke i poštuje pravila Google Play-a za aplikacije za decu (Families).
