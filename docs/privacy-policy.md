# Privacy Policy – Raspored časova

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

Raspored časova ("the app") is developed by Bane (GitHub: mcbane23). This policy explains what the app does with
your data.

## Data the app stores

Everything you enter (children's names, school, timetable, subjects, bell times, calendar events, alarm settings) is
stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. The app has no account, no server and no analytics.

## Data the app collects or shares

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data, and it contains no ads or
third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not request internet access.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** – used only to show the "classes are over" reminder you turn on in Settings.
- **Run at startup** – used only to restore that reminder after the phone restarts.

## Backups

If Android's own device backup is enabled on your phone, the system may include the app's data in your personal
backup (e.g. your Google account backup). This is handled by Android, not by the app.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to delete all data.

## Children

The app is meant to be used by parents and their children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/mcbane23/raspored-casova or contact the developer
through the Google Play listing.

# Politika privatnosti – Raspored časova (srpski)

Sve što uneseš u aplikaciju (imena dece, škola, raspored, predmeti, zvona, događaji, alarm) čuva se **samo na
tvom telefonu**. Aplikacija nema nalog, server ni analitiku, ne šalje i ne deli nikakve podatke i ne koristi
internet. Dozvola za obaveštenja služi samo za podsetnik o kraju nastave, a dozvola za pokretanje pri uključivanju
telefona samo da se taj podsetnik vrati posle restarta. Podaci se brišu deinstalacijom aplikacije ili brisanjem
njene memorije u podešavanjima telefona.
