Class Schedule is a simple app for parents and primary school children in Serbia. Today's classes, the weekly timetable and the school calendar are all in one place, so parents and kids always know when there is school and when there is a break.

• Timetables for the morning and afternoon shift, with exact bell times and one-day changes.
• Serbian school calendar with breaks, holidays and days off (Central Serbia and Vojvodina).
• Parent alarm when the last class ends.
• Morning reminder, school bag reminder and reminders for extracurricular activities.
• Several children, each with their own timetable and shift.
• Timetable sharing by QR code.
• In Serbian and English.

No account, no ads and no internet: all data stays on your phone only.
